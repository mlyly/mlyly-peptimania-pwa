Notifications are opt-in and disabled by default. If you enable them, you acknowledge that
this is the only feature that leaves your device and that it breaks the "local only"
guarantee for the limited data described here.

The notification relay may receive:

- a push endpoint and encryption keys generated by your browser;
- your time zone and the reminder times you choose;
- standard request metadata such as your IP address (not used for analytics);
- in generic mode, only a request to display "a dose is due" — no medication names or doses;
- in detailed mode, an end-to-end encrypted reminder payload that the relay cannot read.

The relay never receives your dose logs, inventory, measurements, notes, backups, or any
account identity. You can disable notifications at any time; the App unsubscribes and asks
the relay to delete the subscription. The relay may retain minimal operational records
(subscription state, reminder timestamps, delivery success or failure) for the shortest
period necessary to operate the service.
